Changes made for Middle C (this is an adaptation of the Work):
  - Selected 30 notes in minor thirds from A0 to C8 and two of the sixteen
    velocity layers (v6 used as "soft", v12 used as "loud").
  - Aligned the two microphone channels and mixed them to mono.
  - Trimmed silence before the hammer strike, shortened each note to about
    2 to 5 seconds with a fade-out, and evened out the level across the keyboard.
  - Re-encoded as 96 kbps MP3.
  These changes are by the Middle C project. Alexander Holm does not endorse
  Middle C or these changes.

Credit line shown in the app:
  Piano samples: Salamander Grand Piano by Alexander Holm (CC-BY 3.0)

All other sounds (electric piano, organ, bass, strings pad, choir, pluck,
drum kit, metronome and the fallback piano) are synthesized in the browser
by js/audio.js and use no recorded samples.
